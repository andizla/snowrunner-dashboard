# Sound credits

Where each sound of SnowRunner Gauges comes from.

| Sound | Files | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The turbo's flutter at full boost, at half and at little, three recordings each | `assets\turbo_high_1.wav` to `_3.wav`, `turbo_mid_1.wav` to `_3.wav`, `turbo_low_1.wav` to `_3.wav` | Nine pieces cut from the sound of one video clip on TikTok: a turbocharged engine pulling, then the flutter as the pedal comes up. The engine's rumble below 450 Hz is filtered out and each piece fades out | not named, as the clip's author wishes | Used with the clip author's permission, given to the mod's author in the clip's comments on 2026-10-07. No open licence: the permission is for this mod |
| The same three sounds where the mod is built without the recordings | none | Made by the mod's code (`SoundMakeBlowOff`, `SoundMakeFlutter` in `src\sound.h`): filtered noise | the mod's author | GPL-3.0-only, as the mod |

The recordings are not under the mod's GPL. Anyone who passes the mod on with them needs the clip author's leave as well, or builds it without the `assets` folder.

A file you put into `sounds\` in the game's `Sources\Bin` folder yourself is yours to answer for: the mod plays it and does not pass it on.
